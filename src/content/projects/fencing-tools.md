I built a few tools for analyzing high-level fencing.

- Scrapes data from several national and international scorekeeping sites and
  analyzes the effects of seeding and upsets at different age levels and
  divisions.
- Automatically downloads livestreams of international competitions from the
  FIE YouTube channel and cuts them into individual bouts for review.
- Scouts and keeps track of opponents in a
  [lightweight notes app](https://fencers.brandonhowe.me).
