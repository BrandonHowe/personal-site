I started the AI Safety club at NJIT with the goal of identifying high-impact students and helping them transition to AI safety. We're running an intro fellowship as well as open meetings. You can check out our resources at [njitaisafety.org](https://njitaisafety.org).

In Fall 2025 I ran SIG Math, where I gave an anthological set of presentations about topics at the intersection of computer science and math. You can view the presentations [here](/sig-math).
